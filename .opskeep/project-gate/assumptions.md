# Belis Bell — Assumptions

Assumptions the builder saw but did not individually confirm. If one is wrong, reopen it:
`gate.py reopen --id PG-xxx --reason "..."`

- **AS-01** English is the launch language. Layouts tolerate text expansion and RTL, but translated content is not part of v1.
- **AS-02** Prices and currency are in Ghana cedis (GHS).
- **AS-03** Delivery is within Ghana. Delivery zones and fees are not designed yet.
- **AS-04** Belis Bell supplies real product photography before launch; placeholders are used meanwhile. The supplied logo is a raster, so a transparent or SVG version and a horizontal lockup are needed before build.
- **AS-05** Usage was set to all day because of heavy contact and ordering, so the store is designed for repeat use as well as first visits.
- **AS-06** The announcement bar and trust counter use mock figures for now. Belis Bell must supply the real free-delivery amount and delivery or customer figure before launch.
- **AS-07** Category names, Washroom Solutions packs and brand spotlights are mock until the real catalogue and supplier relationships are known.
- **AS-08** The address, phone number, email address and opening hours in the mockup are placeholders. They stay mock until Belis Bell supplies the real details.
- **AS-09** Sizes, bulk tiers, specifications, benefit claims and Delivery and Returns text in the mockup are placeholders. Belis Bell supplies the real values; until then they are labelled mock.
- **AS-10** Names, prices, totals, order numbers, delivery details, order dates and statuses in the cart, checkout, confirmation and account mockups are sample values. Nothing in them is real customer or order data.
- **AS-11** Names, emails, order counts, revenue and percentages in the admin and account mockups are sample values, not real data. The admin email admin@belisbell.com and the customer example addresses are placeholders and are never prefilled.

## Decisions confirmed by gate approval (not individually reviewed)

- **PG-040 Products can have several sizes** — A product can offer several sizes (for example 1 litre, 5 litres, 20 litres), each with its own price and stock status. Choosing a size updates the price shown. The server sets every price and total; the page never decides them (CTL-BIZ-001). Sizes are shown but Add to Cart stays switched off until the cart is built.
- **PG-041 Ratings, review counts and sold counts are not shown yet** — The mockup shows a star rating, a review count and a '500+ sold' figure. These are not shown until a real reviews system exists, because they would otherwise be made up. A reviews system needs its own security review (moderation, abuse, fake reviews) and would change the security baseline. Benefit claims such as 'Kills 99.9% of germs' come from the supplier's data sheet and show as mock text until the owner confirms them (PG-038).
- **PG-043 Extra product content and Common Uses photos** — Each product can carry a full description, specifications, how to use, key features, three highlights and a list of common uses. Delivery and Returns text is the same for every product and comes from the owner. Common Uses tiles use owner photos named by use (laundry, bathrooms, kitchens, floors, bins), shared across products. All of this is mock text until supplied. The download of a data sheet appears only when a file exists.
- **PG-044 Header shows a search field on wide screens** — On wide screens the header shows a search field between the links and the account and cart icons, as in the product mockup. On small screens it stays an icon. Search itself is not built yet, so the field is switched off.
- **PG-046 Shop filters work on the server and keep their state in the address** — Category, price range, availability, brand and sort combine. Every choice is checked on the server, so odd values fall back to safe defaults. The address keeps the filters, so a filtered page can be shared or bookmarked and works without JavaScript. Counts beside each option come from the catalogue. Clear Filters resets everything. Page size is 12.
- **PG-047 No star ratings or rating filter; brand filter only with brand data** — The mockup shows star ratings, review counts and a rating filter. These stay out until a real reviews system exists (PG-041). The Brand filter appears only when products carry a brand. The catalogue gets an optional brand field; the mock catalogue uses invented brand names from the mockup. Real brands come from the owner, and photos of other companies' brands need permission (PG-035).
- **PG-048 Shop card extras** — Cards show photo, name, size, price and a stock badge with an icon. The heart (save for later) and Add to Cart are shown but switched off until the cart and saved lists are built. The small quantity stepper in the mockup is left off cards until the cart exists; the product page has one.
- **PG-049 Shop trust rows are the owner's promises** — The hero trust row and the bottom strip (including 'Great Value: Competitive prices') are promises. They show as mock copy until the owner confirms each one, and Secure Payments appears only once live payments are approved (PG-038).
- **PG-051 Categories can have subcategories** — A category can have subcategories, one level deep, each with its own product count. The category page lists them in the filter panel and filters by the chosen one. The mock catalogue uses the subcategory names from the mockup for Cleaning Products only. Real subcategories come from the owner.
- **PG-054 Delivery rules are mock until supplied** — The regions and towns lists, Standard delivery at GHS 20.00 (1 to 3 working days in Accra, 2 to 5 elsewhere) and Express at GHS 40.00 (same day in Accra, order before 12pm) are mock until Belis Bell supplies real rules. The server sets the delivery fee from the address and option (CTL-BIZ-001). The 'agree to Terms and Privacy Policy' line appears only once those pages exist with real text (DEC-005).
- **PG-056 Order updates and tracking** — Updates go by email. The 'Get Updates on WhatsApp' button opens a click-to-chat with the order number (DEC-008); automatic WhatsApp messages need the WhatsApp Business API and would be a separate security change. The five tracking steps are moved by staff in the admin (not built yet); until then the page shows only the steps the system knows (Order Received, Payment Confirmed). 'Download Invoice' is left out until Belis Bell supplies its business and tax details.
- **PG-058 Account scope for the first version** — Payment Methods is not built: payment details stay with Paystack (CTL-PAY-001), and customers choose how to pay on Paystack's page each time. Recurring orders are left for later; version one has one-click Reorder. Account type is Individual or Business, chosen at sign-up (PG-067); institutions still request a quote each time, and staff link an organisation only when repeat buyers need shared access (PG-023). Invoices and Notifications pages appear once there is real content (tax details, email preferences). Total Spent counts only orders whose payment was confirmed.
- **PG-059 Payment channel logos are official marks supplied by the owner** — The row of payment logos (MTN MoMo, Telecel Cash, AirtelTigo Money or AT Money, Visa, Mastercard) uses the official marks, supplied by the owner or from the brands' official brand pages with the owner's agreement, saved as `payments/<name>` slots. Only channels enabled on the Belis Bell Paystack account are shown, using the names the Paystack account shows. Nothing is drawn or recreated. Vodafone Cash is not shown (now Telecel Cash).
- **PG-061 Admin first-version scope and roles** — Build order: Orders, Products and Inventory, Quotes & Bulk Orders, Customers, Users & Roles, then Delivery & Logistics (checkout needs delivery areas). Payments is a read-only view of Paystack transactions. Promotions, Content and Reports come later, and Product Reviews is left out because there is no reviews system (PG-041). Menus and pages depend on the role (owner, sales, fulfilment, content, DEC-007). Total Revenue, the sales chart, Users & Roles and Settings are owner-only. Exports are limited, logged and approved by the owner. Figures count only orders whose payment was confirmed. The profile shows the person's own role.
- **PG-063 Admin sign-in rules** — No Remember me and no Continue with Google on the admin login, because staff sessions are short by design and a third-party sign-in would change the security design. Forgot password is owner-only: staff ask the owner to reset their access. After the password, staff enter an emailed code as customers do (PG-069). The page is not linked from the storefront. The left panel lists only what is built, and the secure-access note states something specific and true, such as that every sign-in is confirmed with an emailed code and sessions expire quickly.
- **PG-065 Customer sign-in details** — A code is required at every sign-in, and 'Remember me' is not shown; a 'trust this device for 30 days' option can replace it if the owner wants fewer codes. Continue with Google and Continue with Apple are left out for now, since they add outside companies as ways in and email codes already cover the need. Email delivery must work (SPF, DKIM and DMARC, CTL-ACCT-001), with a resend button and a Chat on WhatsApp link for people who do not get the code. The panel text is specific and true (for example 'Sign-ins are confirmed with an email code'), and the Exclusive Offers line is left out because promotions are not in the first version.
- **PG-067 Register details** — Business is chosen by the person and starts as unverified: no shared access or special pricing until staff confirm the organisation (CTL-ORG-001), and a Business sign-up also asks for the organisation's name. Passwords need at least 10 characters with no forced symbols, are checked against known leaked passwords and allow passphrases, instead of the mockup's 8 characters with a mix. Delivery information is not asked at sign-up: it is asked at checkout and saved in My Addresses, which collects less personal data. The terms tick box appears only once the Terms and Privacy Policy pages exist with real text (PG-054, DEC-005). The phone number is collected, but only the email is verified for now.
